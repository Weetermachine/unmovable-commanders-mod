Stationary Commander
====================
Commanders cannot move or attack. When armies leave a territory that has a commander on it — whether by attacking, transferring, or airlifting — the commander is left behind on their current territory.

This fundamentally changes how commanders work: instead of a mobile unit that leads your armies across the map, the commander becomes a fixed defensive anchor. Losing your commander's territory means losing the game, so you must fortify and defend it carefully rather than rushing it forward.

How it works
------------
- If you attack from a territory that has your commander on it, your armies attack normally but your commander stays put.
- If you transfer armies out of a commander's territory, the commander remains.
- If you airlift armies from a commander's territory, the commander is not airlifted with them.
- Commanders still defend normally — they fight at full strength when their territory is attacked.
- Commanders can still be killed if their territory is successfully captured.

Tips
----
- Your commander's starting territory is essentially your "base" — losing it means elimination, so stack defenses there.
- You can no longer use your commander as an offensive weapon, so plan your attacks using normal armies only.
- Because your commander can't advance, opponents know exactly where it is at all times. Use terrain and bonus structures to compensate.
- This mod pairs well with large maps where the commander's immobility creates interesting strategic tradeoffs between offense and defense.
